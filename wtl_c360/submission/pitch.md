# Collections C360 — demo pitch

Collections teams need reliable customer context, but the inputs are fragmented across CRM, card, loan, deposit, bureau, contact, and transcript systems. C360 builds a traceable customer view over the complete synthetic Maple release, keeps raw sources immutable, and publishes a separate masked analytics surface.

Agents and analysts can ask approved data or policy questions with visible sources and access controls. Point-in-time features let a team evaluate future promise outcomes without using information from the future. A temporal LightGBM model is compared with a logistic baseline, and demographic attributes are reserved for a separate, small-cell-suppressed fairness audit.

The product is deliberately human-led: consent, hardship cues, disputes, contact limits, and model suggestions route to review; the system does not contact customers or offer payment terms. The demo is transparent about its synthetic data, rule-based identity matching, text masking limits, and observational outcome labels.
